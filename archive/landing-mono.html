Questions

Things people ask

Where does the data come from?
From Nutrislice, the menu system Milton's dining service publishes to. Nothing is typed in by hand, so when the dining hall changes a dish, the app changes with it.
Are the photos the actual dishes?
No. The dining hall doesn't publish photos, so each dish is shown with a stock photo of the same kind of food. The numbers are the real ones.
How accurate is the nutrition info?
It's exactly what the dining service publishes, which is usually right and occasionally odd (a sauce listing every allergen, say). Treat allergen info as a starting point and check with dining staff.
What does "optimal" mean?
Closest to your calorie and macro targets for that meal, with a few rules: being short on protein counts against a plate more than going over, sodium is kept in check, and fiber earns a small bonus.
