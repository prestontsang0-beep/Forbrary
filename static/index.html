    How to measure a serving

    Measures use what's on the line. The hand check is for when you serve yourself.

    • Scoop · ½ cup (4 oz)The big serving spoon on the hot line: sides, rice, pasta, and a scoop of a mixed dish. Hand check: a cupped hand.
    • Small scoop · 2 oz (¼ cup)The salad-bar and deli spoon: tomatoes, peppers, beans, cheese, eggs. Hand check: half a cupped hand; for cheese, about two thumbs.
    • Tongful · about 1 cupOne grab of lettuce or greens with the tongs. Hand check: a fist-sized pile.
    • PiecesChicken, naan, slices, wedges: count them as they're served. Half a piece is half.
    • Ladle · 2 oz · Soup ladle · 8 ozSauces come from the small ladle, soup from the big one (about a cup).
    • Spoonful · 1 TbspDressings, salsa, seeds, toppings. Hand check: your thumb.

    Serving spoons are usually stamped with their size on the handle; if yours says 6 or 8 oz, count it as one and a half or two scoops. Weights are as served. Half-servings on your plate scale the same way: ×½ of 2 pieces is 1 piece, ×½ of a scoop is half a scoop.
